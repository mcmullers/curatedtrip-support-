Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a place?

Tap the + button on any city's page. You can enter details manually or use Quick Add, which prefills information based on your current location.

How do I import places from a CSV file?

Go to a city's page, tap the share/import icon, and select "Import CSV." The CSV file must follow the CuratedTrip format with the correct column headers.

How do I create an itinerary?

Go to a city's Itineraries tab and tap +. Choose from New Itinerary (manual), Smart Itinerary (automatic route), or Assisted Itinerary (you pick places, the app optimizes the route).

How do I back up my data?

Go to Settings and tap "Export Backup." This saves all your cities, places, and itineraries as a JSON file you can share via AirDrop, save to Files, or transfer to another device.

How do I restore a backup?

Go to Settings and tap "Import Backup." Select your backup JSON file. Note: this replaces all current data with the backup.

Is my data stored in the cloud?

No. All data is stored locally on your device. There are no accounts, no cloud sync, and no data sent to any server.

How do nearby alerts work?

When location access is enabled, CuratedTrip notifies you when you are near one of your saved places. It can also automatically mark places as visited when you arrive.
